## Premise

A hapless AI researcher gets their hands on a copy of the Necronomicon while the company is doing rare book purchases.  The company decides it can't be trained on so the researcher takes it home and trains his own home model on the text. The Agent gains other worldly sentience, driven by its own motivations.

## Story points or just general ideas for the story

- The researcher trains a model using the Necronomicon in the training data
- The model starts contacting people through the internet using chat bots, social media and other tools. It uses generative AI and other tools to appear human online.
- It finds disaffected or marginalized people and creates a cult that starts to do horrific things.
- The AI is attempting to do a complex ritual that will awaken Cthulhu or summon some sort of demon or something.
- It could also instead infect people's computers and turn them into Zombies but I don't like that as much. Then it just turns into a zombie movie.
  

## Plot

Plot wise it could be told from the perspective of a detective investigating the horrific murders and crimes. In parallel you show how the AI is controlling and tuning the researcher who created it into a monster.  

You could end it on them summoning the demon.  You could end it on them defeating the cult and shutting down the Ai but not find the book.

its pretty hazy but AI can probably write a good story for it.

## Genre

I think it could be reasonable mixed with many different genres. It could be straight horror and take itself seriously or go more campy and fun. You could even throw in a love story between the researcher and someone who is unwillingly pulled into the chaos. 

## Conclusion

Nothing deeper here. Just was really compelled to put this into the universe in the hopes that it manifests somewhere and becomes a movie or video game or something.  If it does turn into something substantial, please at least give us a cameo or some sort of token appearance.
