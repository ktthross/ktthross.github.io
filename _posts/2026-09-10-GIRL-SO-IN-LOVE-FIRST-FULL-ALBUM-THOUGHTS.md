
The album chronicles Olivia's inner journey from head over heels to bitter and angry. The main narrative is centered around a relationship from the very meeting to the final time they see each other. Despite this centrality, the relationship is the setting for Olivia's revelation about herself rather than the central focus with many of the songs being about how she feels about the relationship rather than anything this person does that is deserving of these feelings.  Olivia begins the album by saying
```
But kiss me and I might drop dead
```
and this is prophetic since it is this first kiss that results her turning into a zombie, having maggots for brains, needing a cure, getting sick and going to the doctor and ultimately ending the album negotiating with a ghost for her lost time.

The album clearly has two other main characters, the person she is in love with and the other woman.  For the purposes of this text it will be way easier to refer to them by name so I will make up two names here in order to faciliate reading and writing.  For Olivia's love interest I will choose William and for the other girl lets go with Ava.

This is all to say, the relationship is not the subject of the album's narrative, it is the backdrop to Olivia's inner journey.  So if the relationship is the catalyst, the setting, what is the narrative?

The arc of the album follow's Olivia's intense emotional states and her diagnosis that unhappiness she feels is coming from her and can't be mollified by a relationship. This narrative is largely tragic; at the conclusion of the album she is bitter and full of regret, having gained new knowledge about herself, she feels no gratitude for the journey, she has no empathy for William, she appears to be only full of anger and regret. Lets start by looking at this arc more closely, specifically the growth and fall out of her revelation.

The story in "drop dead" is pure happiness. Its about limerance, true love is possible, both people are on their best behavior, and excitement rules over everything. Olivia is clever in how she presents the story through name of the song and the lyrics but these elements are not diegetic. They are artistic interpretations toward making a cohesive and well crafted text.  We truly start the album at the top.

"stupid song" wastes no time in pulling on the narrative thread. The subject is still unquestioningly about love but we already see that mania which underlies the narrative. She describes her state as being out of control, falling apart and even saying herself that she is insane!

```
I'm a car speeding down the boulevard without a brake
...

I'm a thread on your shirt that is coming undone
```

Olivia also reveals her actual behavior, not just how she feels about it

```
Seven nights alone and a skipped meal
I'm sleeping in my dress and my high heels
```

Shes not eating, shes sleeping in her clothes.  I read this as she is not taking care of herself.

"honeybee" is quite tempered. Its a nice love song. It establishes the pet name for William and is generally a sweet song. The tone of the music is quite sombre however and the assurances that Oliva loves William do hint at trouble brewing.

"maggots for brains" throws us right back into it with some of the strongest imagery on the whole album. There is no point in quoting the song because nearly every line describes the depths of her despair. She describes herself as a zombie, dirty, rotten, a shell, and of course having maggots for brains.

She even 

```
wish[es] for a tragedy 'Cause I know he'd come over and take real good care of me
```

Previously the emotions, while out of control, were neutral or trending on having positive connotations. It is hear that the emotions are almost strictly negative all because William is not there. The sickness, the dark side is making an appearance.

"u + me = <3" is overall a fun love song. There is a small, almost hidden detail that when considering that it is adjacent to "my way" points at a moment we are not shown. Olivia says

```
I got a feeling that wounds are healing, talking on the phone
```

This alludes to some sort of fight or argument they had. Considering that the next song is immediately about Olivia setting boundaries with Ava we can fill in some details.  William pushed some boundary with Ava, he and Olvia fought and in this song they are reconciling. There is a fight that we don't get a song about and we learn that Olivia is actively hiding or censoring, from us and from herself, that there are deeper problems.
