Part of the ongoing album notes in [`you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love — Raw Thoughts`]({% post_url 2026-06-15-YLPSFAGSIL-RAW-THROUGHTS %}).

This song comes across as a bit of a front and an attempt at self-deception. Olivia is trying to convince herself in this song.

In the very first verse you can see this tension laid bare. Olivia can see the flaws and red flags in some new boy

> I think he was on drugs
> He wasn't smart or funny, I convinced myself he was
> He had a great apartment and a car his parents bought
> I thought that he was perfect

The attraction is superficial by her own admission but this does not stop her from "falling" for this guy.

The pre-chorus comes off as almost sardonic with Olivia starting by saying

> Took a couple months (Took a couple months)
> But now I am secure (Now I am secure)
> I am so evolved (I am so evolved)

It took her a whole couple of months to become secure and evolved when the preceding song was literally about how she would rather suffer in a relationship than change.

She follows this with an almost villainous declaration that

> Now I ask for more and more and more and more and more

The chorus comes off as genuine. Olivia is trying to set boundaries for herself so she doesn't get hurt as bad.

> They seem so desperate for loving, but, baby, I'm not

> Gave my heart with zero stipulations
> Now I take careful consideration

> Past mistakes are just new information
> These days, I've got expectations

Her assertions are immediately undercut by the next verse, however. She returns to the dating scene, seemingly just as naive as before

> So I hit the new year like a single girl at a Vegas bar
> Rocking my mini dress with a vodka cran' and an open heart

This contradicts that she is now more careful with her heart and that she is not desperate.

> Yeah, I've got hope, yeah, I've got drive, I will not lose my faith

Going to single bars with hope and faith sounds exactly like the actions of a person desperate to find love.

This is capped with a spoken line that is self-aware and derisive

> Don't think my future husband's at this bar in Silver Lake

The entire song comes across as the actions and feelings of a person that is conflicted, struggling and putting themselves out there too early. Half right but still hasn't learned their lesson.
