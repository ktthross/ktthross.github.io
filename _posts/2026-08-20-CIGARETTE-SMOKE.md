Part of the ongoing album notes in [`you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love — Raw Thoughts`]({% post_url 2026-06-15-YLPSFAGSIL-RAW-THROUGHTS %}).

The final track and a depressing resolution to the narrative.

We get a description of how her SO left and presumably the events immediately before they were crying at LAX in *less*.  This feels like a companion to *less* in a lot of ways.

> It's a real quiet house
> With the shower left on
> Five beers in the fridge and the second car's gone
> ...
> You'll be miles away tomorrow

This certainly suggests that they left fast and even deceived Olivia. They turned on the shower and then escaped out the back door with a single beer.

Reading the text we see Olivia specifically referencing the other girl from *my way*

> I regret you
> And what I let slide
> I resent you
> For taking her side

It makes me think that the cigarette smoke in the opening line is an indicator that her SO has been with this other woman rather than with Olivia.  The narrative of the song itself seems to be that her SO comes home smelling of cigarette smoke. Olivia knows that her so has ignored her boundaries and been hanging out with this other person.  Her SO says that they will go take a shower to get the smell off but rather than shower they just turn it on and then leave, unwilling to be with Olivia anymore.

There is a resolution in this song which we did not get in *less*.  In *less* she is unwilling to let go of the relationship but here we see that she has hit her limit

> Some nights can be
> So fucking lonely
> But it's better than begging for you to stand up for me, honeybee

she wold rather be lonely than begging for what she needs in the relationship.  While ultimately the song has a said ending with Olivia wishing for the memories to go dark, it is satisfying that she is able to decide that she has had enough.

One thing I find surprising is in the chorus, Olivia saying

> Tell me something honest so the memories turn dark

I wonder what Olivia thinks her SO is hiding.  I posited that her SO was lying in *less* when they said they loved her which is why they needed to leave, but here I read this as Olivia knowing that is a lie because if they loved her they would have respected her boundaries.

She needs that honesty so that she can let the memories go dark since its easier thinking that they never loved her.

The album ends with Olivia saying

> Go dark
