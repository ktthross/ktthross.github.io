Olivia real gets to the heart of the conflict and catalyst for the relationship breaking down. She also sets out the stakes.

She needs a man that is mor confident and clear about their intentions. Olivia states some really powerful sentiments, that she is an "anchor in the ocean and that she will never leave". This echoes the outcome in `less` in which it is her lover that is leaving.

We also see that she is isolated again. She is not with the person she loves, she is alone watching TV wishing they would be here to see how much she cares. This is such a strong disconnect.

> So, I'm patient, you're learning, pretend it's not hurting, oh (Oh)

> But nothing's quite enough, when I know that to gеt it, I begged
> Yeah, to gеt it, I begged

Olivia is restless and not satisfied with the situation even though she is trying to convince herself that her love is just learning how to be in the relationship.  Ultimately, she finds that what she gets in the relationship is not enough because she has to beg to get it.

Olivia says "I feel trapped inside my life" which matches the original assertion from her friends in `stupid song` that love is a cage.

There is strong symbols of water throughout the song. "well of needs", "anchor in the ocean", "fight back the waves", "snow on mountains", "words melt it away" "penny in a fountain".  The melting also feels like a call back to purple.

Olvia is stuck, trapped and unwilling or unable to move. Clinging like the anchor and the snow.  She is not getting the reassurance she craves, she is being melted away by careless words and hoping things will change.  She describes her lover as being full of dread.

In this song she even characterizes herself as an endless well of needs. She recognizes the problems both with herself and her lover.
