Part of the ongoing album notes in [`You Look Pretty Sad For A Girl So In Love — Raw Thoughts`]({% post_url 2026-06-15-YLPSFAGSIL-RAW-THROUGHTS %}).

A heartbreaking song to be sure and full of conflict and tension. Olivia expresses her desire to be loved less if it means her SO will stay with her. This is contrasted by her depression and inability to feel joy in the relationship. It stirs a genuine question of what love means.

If her SO loves her then wouldn't they stay and try to help her get better rather than leave her? If you love someone you would go through the bad times as well as the good times since love ostensibly transcends these sorts of worldly problems.

However if the SO is the source of the depression, as she asserts in `the cure` and `what's wrong with me`, then leaving her is the right choice because they are the source of the ailment. The narrative up to this point actually makes the SO heroic in this case because they are willing to do what it takes to make Olivia happy.

It could also be a convenient excuse on the part of the SO. They are unhappy and it's easier to say "I love you, which is why you need to leave" rather than speaking the truth that Olivia is miserable to be around.

With this narrative thread from the other character in the song, the song itself is really about Olivia's inability to let go. Despite articulating sentiments

> Body and head protesting
> My stomach's in knots, I don't wanna talk

> But we didn't laugh much this time
> Our trip to Big Sur only confirmed
> This isn't what it should feel like

> And I could try convincing you they're just intrusive thoughts
> But you've seen me truly happy, so you know right now I'm not

Even with the acknowledgement that this relationship does not make her happy, she would rather be unhappy with someone who loves her less.

The song is definitively about the end of the relationship from the final lyrics of the song

> If loving me means crying on the curb at LAX
> Well, then I guess
> I wish, I wish, I wish you loved me less
> If loving me means saying, "Babe, I think this is the end"

but is also not about the end of the relationship at all. Olivia is holding on to despair and unhappiness, yearning for things to not change.
