Sounding contributors

Volume and phase, resolved: each contributor's coefficient sets both. Its magnitude becomes that tone's gain; a negative sign is realized as a π phase flip — done here simply by feeding the (signed) coefficient straight into the oscillator's gain, since -A sin(2πft) = A sin(2πft+π). The whole chord is then rescaled to a fixed RMS so louder cutoffs (more notes, more energy) don't just mean louder playback.
